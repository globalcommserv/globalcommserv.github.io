GCS and Nationwide IT Services Launch GCNIS Joint Venture

GCS and Nationwide IT Services formed GCNIS, an SBA Mentor-Protégé Joint Venture created to deliver secure, innovative, and cost-effective IT solutions to federal customers.

Read More

GCNIS is a HUBZone and Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business. The joint venture combines NIS expertise in cloud computing, software development, and advanced analytics with GCS capabilities in IT infrastructure, networking, and cybersecurity.

Together, the companies offer a single, accountable partner for enterprise IT modernization with the scale and technical depth of a large business and the agility and responsiveness of a small business.

July 2025

GCS Awarded GSA OASIS+ Contract

GCS was awarded a position on GSA OASIS+ in the Management & Advisory Domain under both the HUBZone and Women-Owned Small Business tracks.

Read More

OASIS+ is GSA's premier governmentwide, multiple-award IDIQ contract for complex professional services. Through the Management & Advisory Domain, GCS can support program and project management, strategic planning, organizational transformation, acquisition and financial management, process improvement, performance management, policy analysis, and advisory requirements.

The award expands federal customers' access to GCS through flexible HUBZone and WOSB acquisition pathways.
