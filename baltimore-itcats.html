Frequently Asked Questions

What services are available through ITCATS?

The contract supports IT consulting, systems, software, cybersecurity, infrastructure, project management, technical staffing, and operational support.

Who can use the contract?

Baltimore County departments may use the vehicle in accordance with County procurement procedures.

Is this a State contract?

No. This is a Baltimore County local government contract vehicle.
