Company Overview

Reliable delivery in complex government environments

GCS helps defense, civilian, and state and local government organizations translate mission and business requirements into practical, executable solutions. Our teams combine technical expertise, disciplined program execution, and hands-on management oversight in environments where performance, security, and accountability matter.

Our delivery approach is grounded in recognized practices including PMBOK®, ITIL®, DevSecOps, an ISO 9001:2015-certified Quality Management System, and CMMC Level 2-compliant cybersecurity practices.

Leadership

Joaneane A. Smith

Founder, President & Chief Executive Officer

Joaneane A. Smith brings more than 25 years of experience in management consulting, enterprise information systems, and federal contracting. She has led technology implementation and business transformation initiatives for organizations including the U.S. Department of the Navy, the U.S. Department of the Army, Smurfit-Stone Container, and the University of Rochester.

Under her leadership, GCS has grown into a trusted provider of IT and professional services. She remains personally committed to quality, customer success, small-business advancement, and long-term partnerships that deliver measurable value.
