GCS MDA SHIELD Overview

Supporting Layered Defense Missions

The Missile Defense Agency Scalable Homeland Innovative Enterprise Layered Defense contract encompasses a broad range of work areas designed to accelerate delivery of innovative capabilities to the warfighter.

GCS brings disciplined execution, technical support, cybersecurity, engineering, and program management capabilities to mission requirements competed through the vehicle.
