About GCNIS

One Accountable Partner for Enterprise IT Modernization

GCNIS is an SBA-approved Mentor-Protégé Joint Venture established by Global Commerce and Services, LLC and Nationwide IT Services.

The joint venture combines the scale, technical depth, and enterprise experience of a larger organization with the agility, responsiveness, and customer focus of a small business. GCNIS is a HUBZone and Economically Disadvantaged Woman-Owned Small Business.

NIS contributes expertise in cloud computing, software development, and advanced analytics, while GCS provides strong capabilities in IT infrastructure, networking, cybersecurity, and federal program execution. Together, the companies deliver end-to-end solutions that support mission-critical operations across the federal enterprise.
