GCS GSA MAS Overview

A Simplified Path to Technology Services

GSA’s Multiple Award Schedule is one of the Federal Government’s most widely used acquisition vehicles. It gives eligible customers access to prequalified vendors, established terms, and streamlined ordering procedures.

Through SIN 54151S, GCS offers information technology services that support modernization, infrastructure, applications, data, and mission operations.

GCS Services Under GSA MAS

IT Services SIN 54151S

GCS is authorized to provide cloud services, cognitive computing, conversion and implementation support, database planning and design, IoT, IT project management, migration, network services, programming, resource and facilities management, and systems analysis, design, and implementation.

The MAS program can shorten procurement cycles, provide flexible labor categories, and give customers a practical path to modern IT and professional services under established contract terms.
