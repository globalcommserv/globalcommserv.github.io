GCS OASIS+ Overview

A Best-in-Class Path to Mission Success

GSA OASIS+ is a governmentwide suite of multiple-award indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity contracts designed to streamline the acquisition of complex professional services. Through the Management & Advisory Domain, GCS supports agencies with program and project management, strategic planning, organizational transformation, acquisition support, governance, performance management, and business process improvement.

Our two prime awards give customers access to GCS through both HUBZone and WOSB acquisition pathways while supporting Federal socioeconomic contracting goals.
