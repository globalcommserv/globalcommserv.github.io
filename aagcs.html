About AAGCS

Purpose-Built for GSA 8(a) STARS III

AAGCS is a joint venture between Global Commerce and Services, LLC and Orgro established to perform work under the GSA 8(a) STARS III contract.

Through STARS III, AAGCS provides innovative information technology and emerging technology solutions to federal agencies across a broad range of mission environments. The joint venture supports systems engineering and integration, software development and modernization, cybersecurity, cloud and infrastructure services, network engineering, data management, and IT program support.

AAGCS combines complementary experience in technology delivery, federal mission support, program execution, and small-business responsiveness through one accountable prime contractor.
