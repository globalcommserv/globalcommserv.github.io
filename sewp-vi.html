About SEWP VI

A federal acquisition path for mission technology requirements

NASA SEWP is a Government-Wide Acquisition Contract (GWAC) available to federal agencies and approved federal contractors. SEWP VI supports the acquisition of information technology, communications, audio-visual solutions, and related services.

GCS holds a Category C contract focused on mission-based service solutions. Category C provides customers a streamlined path to acquire services that support a specific mission, program, system, application, network, or operational environment.

Ordering

How to Obtain a GCS Quote

Agency ordering procedures vary. The issuing agency determines its internal purchase request and delivery/task order process; NASA SEWP PMO reviews, processes, and tracks orders issued under the vehicle.

  1. 1

    Define the requirement

    Develop the statement of work, performance work statement, specifications, labor needs, period of performance, and any security or technical requirements.

  2. 2

    Request a quote

    Use the NASA SEWP online RFQ process or the ordering method authorized by your contracting office. GCS will respond with contract-available services and pricing.

  3. 3

    Complete fair opportunity

    The ordering contracting officer applies the fair-opportunity procedures required by FAR 16.505(b) and the SEWP contract.

  4. 4

    Issue the order

    The agency procurement office issues the delivery or task order. NASA SEWP PMO does not issue customer orders; it processes and tracks orders issued by the ordering agency.

Contract Compliance

Fair Opportunity & RFQ Compliance

GCS recognizes NASA SEWP VI as a multiple-award Government-Wide Acquisition Contract. Contractors are provided fair opportunity at the individual order level as appropriate under FAR 16.505(b), including use of SEWP RFQ tools. The issuing procurement office maintains its order-selection documentation.

For RFQs and Market Research Requests, GCS will quote only services and solutions available on our SEWP contract and will comply with the pricing, quote-validity, response, and Contract Holder User Manual requirements applicable to the request. If GCS cannot fully respond to a formal RFQ, we will follow SEWP requirements for a No Bid unless partial quotes are expressly permitted.
