GCS NASA SEWP VI Overview

An Enterprise IT Acquisition Path

NASA Solutions for Enterprise-Wide Procurement (SEWP) VI is designed to support Federal acquisition of information technology products and related services.

GCS uses the vehicle to help customers access mission-aligned technology solutions through an established governmentwide procurement channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can agencies buy through SEWP VI?

The vehicle supports information technology products and related services, including hardware, software, cloud-related offerings, and associated services.

Is GCS a prime contractor?

Yes. GCS holds a prime contract on NASA SEWP VI.

Who can use NASA SEWP?

Federal agencies and authorized customers may use the vehicle in accordance with NASA SEWP ordering procedures.
